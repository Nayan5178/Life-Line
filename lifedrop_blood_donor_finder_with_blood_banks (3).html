How LifeDrop works

Three simple steps to help connect people in need with people ready to help.

01

Search by blood group

Select the required blood group and add a city or area to find matching registered profiles.

02

Check location & contact

Review the donor's listed area and approximate distance, then call or message only if the donor has consented to be contacted.

03

Donate with care

Confirm availability and contact a licensed blood bank or healthcare professional for compatibility and eligibility guidance.
